question; the primary comparison has not been measured and its detailed protocol
is not yet frozen. No confirmed birth, identified population hidden-protostar
fraction, physical lifetime measurement or validated detector is asserted.

The blog post lives in `_posts/2026-10-11-hidden-protostars-research-program.md`.

are unchanged. The interactive example uses invented 20% clump occurrence and

## Interpretation

E15 counterpart counts are consumed historical evidence, not untouched validation.
SPICY candidate-YSO overlap shares infrared information and does not supply
independent birth labels. E11 estimator spreads and corrected-share scenarios
are descriptive sensitivity, not confidence bounds or measured lifetimes.
E27/E28 failed the two specified incremental-value checks, not every possible
image approach. N20 and c6828 remain stopped. E29–E160 remain a separate archive.

The seven-day program must register its footprint, band, match rules, quality
thresholds, estimator, spatial dependence treatment, uncertainty, decision
thresholds and cost caps before the new outcome is read. A positive, null or
insufficient-evidence conclusion is acceptable. A follow-up list contains at most
20 supported association candidates, never a promised list of newborn stars.
