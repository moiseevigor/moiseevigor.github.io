What the ordering means

A solid box is an input; a dashed box is a local computation. The two marked boxes are the adjacent pair being exchanged. Vertical order is lexical scope. It is not evaluation time or a proof-dependency edge. Binder names may repeat: an address includes its state and field path.

Each exchange decreases the number of let-before-input pairs by one. Inputs retain their relative order, as do computations. The body and domain changes are capture avoiding. The inverse consumes the recorded steps in reverse order, checking each intermediate owner. Removing or reordering a residual does not establish source recovery.

The view retains one small control, with at most 4,096 source rows, 4,096 native rows, 4,096 steps and 128 binder rows per column. Constructor inspection retains one selected node and at most three children. This does not establish a bound for all browser memory or a paged normalization of arbitrary proofs.
