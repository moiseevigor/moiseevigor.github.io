Visual notation manual · Checks and limits

Equality transport moves a value between fibers of a family. In the kernel, a motive may depend on both the endpoint and the equality witness:

\[ C : (b:A)\to(a=b)\to\mathrm{Sort}_v,\qquad p:C(a,\mathrm{refl}_a),\qquad h:a=b, \qquad \mathrm{Eq.rec}(p,h):C(b,h). \]

For Eq.ndrec, the family ignores the witness. Eq.mp transports between equal types; Eq.mpr uses the reversed equality. These wrappers are expanded by a bounded, kernel-checked template. The square represents typed transport, not a branch in a proof or a recovered tactic.
