The source-use rule is unchanged: connect an ordered input to its exact source producer, retaining local-definition paths. The viewport now loads selected call records and a page of consumer connections rather than a complete topology. Layout and colors carry no mathematical metric.

Count hidden structure without loading it

$$\begin{aligned}E_{\mathrm{in}}&=\sum_{c\in S}\deg_{\mathrm{in}}(c)-E_{SS},\\ E_{\mathrm{out}}&=\sum_{p\in S}\deg_{\mathrm{out}}(p)-E_{SS}.\end{aligned}$$

Each input is owned by its consumer. Selected input rows determine the internal edges and incoming boundary; recorded reverse degrees determine the outgoing count. Consumer rows load separately, eight at a time. Internal edges are excluded from a producer's outgoing list by their exact reverse-index ordinals. No scan of every hidden consumer is needed to request a distant page.

Coverage and limits

The larger packet has 6,479 stored expression nodes. Indexing its occurrence routes separately is a deliberate scale test, not a compressed representation of all shared proof instances. Its index grows with the number of call occurrences. The source-only layer does not recover tactics, prove premise necessity or provide new typing observations.

The reader retains at most eight call records, one four-row call-page cache, one 32-row reverse-page cache and two branch records per cursor. Connection lists show eight rows. Native types retain their original evidence owners and existing separate bounds. Source-only selections use bounded original paths and explicitly refuse computed-type selection.
