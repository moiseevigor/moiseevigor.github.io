Coverage and limits

The larger packet has 6,479 stored expression nodes. Indexing its occurrence routes separately is a deliberate scale test, not a compressed representation of all shared proof instances. Its index grows with the number of call occurrences. The source-only layer does not recover tactics, prove premise necessity or provide new typing observations.

The reader retains at most eight call records, one four-row call-page cache, one 32-row reverse-page cache and two branch records per cursor. Connection lists show eight rows. Native types retain their original evidence owners and existing separate bounds. Source-only selections use bounded original paths and explicitly refuse computed-type selection.
