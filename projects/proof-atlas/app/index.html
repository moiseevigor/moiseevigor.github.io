What this does not mean

Distance, color, and cluster layout describe a visualization. They are not invariants of a mathematical theorem, nor a measure of proof quality.

The graph is a measured, anonymized projection of the SE(2) Lean theorem CrossingData.crossing. The mathematical idea is an editorial interpretation, not an automatically verified compression.
