What this does—and doesn't—claim

These diagrams provide a proposed visual grammar for the complete Lean.Expr constructor algebra. They do not yet constitute a certified translation of the entire Lean surface grammar, a tactic-language visualizer, or a proof-net calculus for every theorem. Terms still need typed boundaries, references and environments; scope cannot be inferred from mere visual proximity.

The initial research benchmark publishes the structure and exported term payloads of CrossingData.crossing. The Lean.Expr reference and our exhaustive CoreGIR exporter define the scope of the twelve rules.
