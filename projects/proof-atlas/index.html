The established result

The two extracted inventories cover 739 and 1,359 declarations. In the working corpus, 670,018,056 expanded expression occurrences are represented by 1,277,896 shared nodes, retaining ordered children, payloads and contextual binding.

Five article proof blocks link to complete supporting compiled proofs. The nine numbered statements retain their audited status: five partial, conditional counterparts and four unformalized. Complete corpus coverage does not mean that the whole article is formalized.
