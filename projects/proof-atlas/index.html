Proof Atlas

A geometric language for Lean proofs.

Established structural result

The two extracted inventories cover 739 and 1,359 declarations. In the working corpus, 670,018,056 expanded expression occurrences are represented by 1,277,896 shared nodes, retaining ordered children, payloads and contextual binding.

Five article proof blocks link to complete supporting compiled proofs. The nine numbered statements retain their audited status: five partial, conditional counterparts and four unformalized. Complete corpus coverage does not mean that the whole article is formalized.

Reference and evidence

The Lean language reference separates kernel expressions, universes, syntax trees and major surface-language families. The coverage report records exact inventories, validation and the boundaries of the article correspondence.

Graph layout is a reading aid. Mathematical explanations remain authored interpretations. Original tactic scripts remain private; public packet checks do not constitute an independent project-source kernel rebuild.
