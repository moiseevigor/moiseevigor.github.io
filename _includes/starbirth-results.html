Measured catalogue association

2,465 clumps have an infrared counterpart

12.8% of 19,219 searched catalogue-prestellar clumps

0–100% of the searched sample · 22/24 μm counterparts

Why it matters. This gives us a defined population in which to investigate hidden activity. The other 16,754 have no counterpart in this comparison; that does not establish that they contain no protostar.

Limit. An object along the same sightline may lie in front of or behind the clump. Physical membership remains unverified.
