## Required gates

1. **Scope:** name the Hi-GAL prestellar population, the physical question and
   how the result would change the conclusion. Reject work without that link.
2. **Prospective protocol:** freeze the hypothesis, target population, control,
   primary estimator, comparison, uncertainty method, exclusions, pass/fail
   criterion, stopping condition and compute/storage caps before readout.
   Preserve the original registration and all amendments. Never mark all
   hypotheses preregistered by default or choose the primary after results.
3. **Truth and denominator:** retain every requested object, unknown indicator,
   missing observation and association alternative. Separate catalogue labels,
   proxy support, physical membership, novelty and temporal birth. Scores are
   not class probabilities without calibration. Dust clouds and stars are not
   mutually exclusive classes.
4. **Confounding:** test column density/extinction, distance, mass, temperature,
   surface density, selection and neighbouring sources. A 60-arcsecond offset
   alone does not remove clump-correlated reddening. A latent-class model must
   test identifiability and conditional-dependence sensitivity; agreement with
   the old 19% estimate is not its success criterion.
5. **Comparisons:** use the same objects, missingness support and review cost.
   Include simple physical/tabular baselines with mass and distance. Keep
   development, calibration, consumed references and untouched evaluation
   separate. A proxy-conditioned metric is not population precision/recall.
6. **Claims:** report the approximately 9–25% estimator point-value spread and
   approximately 45–55% corrected-share scenarios as descriptive sensitivity,
   not a confidence interval, lower bound or physical lifetime measurement.
   Do not quote an unqualified contamination/lifetime result before a defensible
   column-matched control or validated multi-indicator model exists.
7. **Reproducibility:** every headline must have checked-in generation code,
   input and sample hashes, uncertainty assumptions and independently checked
   output. Cache reuse must verify sample identity and returned target geometry;
   changed coordinates or membership must fail closed. Preserve old artifacts.
8. **Bounded work:** cached, offline analysis first. One registered scientific
   question per work unit, at most 30 minutes and 64 MiB new outputs initially,
   with a 5 GiB disk reserve. No open-ended model sweep, repeated failed network
   route or expansion to a new survey. An exhausted limit produces a documented
   incomplete result, not an automatic successor experiment.
9. **Checkpoint:** assess the scientific result against the frozen question,
   record any changed conclusion, and commit the coherent verified change.
   Do not rank narrative catch-all proposals by the number of phases they list.
   Stop after a failed question unless a distinct, justified on-goal test is
   registered. Operational verification alone is not scientific progress.
10. **Publication:** retain corrected core findings and their caveats together.
    Restore the designated Atlas to the E28 core state before selectively
    publishing reviewed core corrections. Keep the tranche archive separate.
    Preserve identity/sharing; do not publish queued E156–E160 updates. Respect
    the rejected E155 readback and E121 upload restrictions.
