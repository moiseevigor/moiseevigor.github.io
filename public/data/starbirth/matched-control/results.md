## Model context and scope

[Marsh et al. (2015), equations 15–16](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.08699v1)
derive marginal posterior uncertainties from the diagonal of an inverse
curvature matrix. Independent prior occupation variables do not supply posterior
bin independence. The [Hi-GAL PPMAP release paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1707.03808v1)
describes marginal uncertainty cubes and separates random errors from omitted
systematic effects. These published statements motivate the covariance audit;
they do not measure correlations for our 1,000 clumps.
