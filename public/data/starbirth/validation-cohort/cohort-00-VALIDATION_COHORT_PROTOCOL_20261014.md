# Prospective independent-evidence cohort: frozen design

Human authorization: build the validation cohort and audit ownership/coverage,
14 October 2026. This unit constructs a review frame, not physical truth or a
new model. All previous feature values, rankings and chemistry outcomes have
already been inspected. There is no untouched test of those outcomes.

## Population, eligibility and ownership

Retain every one of the 19,328 nearby catalogue-prestellar parent designations,
exact native molecular alternatives and the existing indivisible association
groups. Reproduce parent identity from the raw Hi-GAL catalogue. Freeze the
complete input bytes in `artifacts/validation_cohort_registration_20261014.json`.
Use only verified core/current-program ledgers; no E29–E160 inputs or execution.

An entire group is excluded from the prospective adjudication frame if any
member has a qualifying consumed proxy, E13 sample membership, SPICY candidate
membership, consumed MALT90/ALMAGAL membership or matched-control membership.
This deliberately conservative restriction protects the stopped occurrence
comparison and prevents old outcome evidence being called fresh. All excluded
groups stay in the population ledger, with exact reasons. Prior use of unlabelled
features is disclosed; this is a prospective assessment of frozen scores on
new external outcomes, not an untouched representation-training experiment.

No independent-truth manifest is supplied. Every physical label therefore
remains unknown and presently certified validation eligibility is false. The
prospective frame means permission to seek/adjudicate future independent
evidence; it does not certify membership, youth or independence of that evidence.

Separate projected WISE coordinates, model-quality temporal features, the earlier
less complete temporal screen, molecular beam ambiguity, native source IDs and
physical ownership. A unique unshared molecular row is geometric support only.
No retained WISE alternative/astrometric-error contract is invented. Three already
inspected native cases retain their failed association/quality context. Survey
absence in a cache is not verified sky-footprint exclusion or physical absence.
Native molecular edge records, including neighbours outside the parent, remain
downloadable. Independent-truth absence refers only to this registered inventory.

## Bounded probability review design

Use the existing group IDs, never split their members. Cross four states of the
frozen original embedding and nonlinear all-parent review lists (both, embedding
only, nonlinear only, neither) with any member having the four registered temporal
features. These eight group-level strata prevent inspecting only model-favoured
sources and expose complementary-view selection. Sample at most 30 groups in each
stratum by ascending SHA-256 of a pre-read random seed and exact group ID; tie
by group ID. No adaptive redistribution, replacement or brightness gate. Export
every member of each selected group, with stratum size, sampled groups, inclusion
fraction n/N and inverse fraction N/n. Cap at 240 groups/480 member records; fail
closed if exceeded. The random seed is fixed in the registration before readout.
Group weights apply to each member when estimating parent totals. The realized
priority sample is reproducible; probabilities refer to the seeded randomized
design, not to arbitrary human choice of cases. A small or empty stratum stays
small or empty. Preserve unselected frame members and all exclusions.

Review at most 40 groups/day for six days. Record native observation identifier,
instrument/beam/epoch, sensitivity/coverage, competing emitters, velocity/distance
membership, independent physical diagnostic, citation and prior consumption for
each adjudication. Reviewers should be blinded to scores and stratum; the public
queue exposes selection for reproducibility, so a reviewer given that queue is
not blinded. A separate review interface/held-out outcome file is required before
any performance readout. This unit collects no new data and fills no labels.

Accept a positive only with independently resolved protostellar evidence and
defensible parent membership; a catalogue candidate, red colour, same-survey
chemistry flag, fragmentation or a projected maser alone is insufficient. A
negative needs adequate diagnostic sensitivity/coverage and an explicit scope;
non-detection alone stays unknown. Retain unresolved cases, prior evidence and
disagreements. Birth requires time-resolved collapse/transition evidence beyond
ordinary variability; no cohort classification establishes birth or novelty.

## Evaluation stop and power

No classifier fit, threshold adjustment or precision/recall calculation in this
unit. The old 982-per-method parent lists remain unchanged. Future evaluation
requires independent adjudication, spatial grouping/buffer and fixed endpoints;
include sampled unselected sources. Weighted counts need known inclusion
probabilities; recall cannot be inferred from a shortlist alone. Frame exclusions
limit generalization; never claim parent-wide recall from this restricted frame.
Unresolved adjudications require explicit bounds/sensitivity, not negative filling.

Before any physical outcome readout, freeze a separate paired power calculation
using plausible prevalence, method-discordance, sky clustering and sensitivity
scenarios. This 240-group ceiling is an operational review budget, not proof of
statistical power. If independent positive/negative truth, source ownership or
representative coverage cannot be established within one week, conclude
insufficient validation and stop; do not relax eligibility to obtain a result.

## Verification and limits

Worker and separately implemented checker: one CPU, no GPU/network, each at most
600 seconds, sampled RSS 256 MiB, respective output stages 4 MiB and 1 MiB,
5 GiB reserve. Combined guarded time <=1,200 seconds. Check all IDs, joins,
edge/native identities, group eligibility, seeded sampling, inclusion fractions,
coverage cross-tabs and every output hash. Verified upstream derivations remain
upstream evidence; numerical checking does not independently validate physics.
Publication is compact exact committed bytes <=16 MiB; preserve old data, app
rankings, private access, formation explorer and program-only progress placement.
