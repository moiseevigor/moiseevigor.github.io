# Cached radio/dense-gas velocity compatibility — one fixed regional marker

13 October 2026. A distinct source-specific comparison within the accepted
multi-survey program. The previous projected result found known maser
330.878-0.367 12.520 arcsec from HIGALBM330.8814-0.3656 but 17.501 arcsec
from its best infrared point. It therefore does not support that point at 1 arcsec.
This unit asks a different question: whether the nearby radio gas is compatible
in reported velocity with the fixed dense-gas record AG1532. A contradiction
would weaken regional attribution; compatibility would add conditional gas
context, never identify the selected star or establish physical clump membership.

## Fixed cases, estimand and consumption

Primary is exactly one case: HIGALBM330.8814-0.3656, AG1532 and native radio
ordinal `VIII/96/catalog.dat:000307`. No substitute molecular row or maser is
selected after readout. The same other two original cases/records are retained
as secondary context (AG3005, AG3227); their nearest markers are already known
to be 103.458 and 79.066 arcsec from their clump centres. They are not verified
unrelated controls and cannot become an expanded primary. Preserve all native
molecular rows within 30 arcsec of each centre, every fixed-ID instance and
all original source alternatives. Duplicate exact fixed IDs stop identity
processing rather than arbitrarily choosing a velocity component.

The primary binary descriptive statistic is whether the registered two dense-
gas bands and the historical maser range have a strictly positive **common**
intersection at zero shift. Pairwise overlaps alone are insufficient. Success
requires an eligible primary record and a positive common interval; no compatible
band yields false, while missing/invalid fits, geometry or a numerical boundary
yield unknown/null, not a physical negative. The sole primary denominator is one,
explicitly selected after the earlier geometry, not three or a population rate.

This is retrospective, consumed source evidence: case selection, native radio
measurements, chemistry references and model-input line widths have already
been inspected. The new comparison rule is frozen before this join/readout;
no fresh representative held-out physical truth or untouched observations are
claimed. No classifier, label, representation, score, review budget or original
19,328-ID/19,066-unknown ledger is changed. Neither this unit nor the previous

## Measurement and uncertainty contract

Use only hash-bound original cached CSV `cache/n1/malt90_rathborne2016.csv`
and the verified original MMB catalogue/records. Keep every literal native field,
string identifier and source-row ordinal. VizieR `recno` is not an independent
physical identity. Parse only the selected/nearby molecular measurements; no
population reclassification, N20 fit or original archival experiment is rerun.

Choose N2H+ and H13CO+ before readout. Require native detection `Y`, respectively
`NG=3` (resolved hyperfine fit) and `NG=1` (single Gaussian), finite velocity,
positive width and finite nonnegative quoted velocity/width errors. Retain
rounded zero quoted errors explicitly; they are not exact physical errors.
Keep missing/bad fields and fit/detection reasons. Other modes remain contextual
native data, never silently accepted. No post-read choice of a more favourable
tracer. In particular, three N2H+ hyperfine components are not three gas clouds.

For each accepted tracer, define a descriptive band centred on its reported
Vlsr, with halfwidth `0.5*FWHM + 3*e_Vlsr + 1.5*e_FWHM + 0.06` km/s.
The width term describes gas spread, while the linear error addition avoids
assuming error independence. The 0.06 padding represents half the reported
0.11-km/s MALT90 channel plus 0.005 rounding allowance. Compare to the MMB
reported `[VL-0.14, VH+0.14]` km/s range: half a 0.18-km/s channel plus 0.05
native rounding allowance. Exact raw limits and MX/SC peaks remain available.
These are declared conventions, **not validated confidence/error bounds**.
Masers can be displaced and variable; reported min/max span historical epochs.

Keep primary zero shift. Show secondary shifts -1, 0 and +1 km/s applied only
to the radio band; these are arbitrary declared calibration-sensitivity
scenarios, not estimated offsets or guaranteed systematic bounds. All three
intervals must share overlap. Absolute signed overlap <=1e-10 km/s is numerically
indeterminate. Independent arithmetic must agree within1e-9 km/s. The literal
published velocity comparison does not reconstruct Doppler/LSR zero points,
rest-frequency/epoch conventions or validate cross-survey calibration. No
stellar velocity exists in these inputs. Even robust conditional compatibility
cannot establish infrared-emitter ownership, distance, youth, membership or birth.
The 30-arcsec contextual radius and 38-arcsec molecular beam are not clump boundaries.

## Source definitions consulted before this readout

[Rathborne et al., sections 3–4](https://researchonline.ljmu.ac.uk/id/eprint/4139/1/MALT90_catalog.pdf)
describes dust-peak spectra, angular/spectral resolution, hyperfine fitting and
catalogue velocity/error fields. NG=3 represents the hyperfine model; NG=2
represents combined unconstrained profiles. The beam and selected dense-gas
population limit physical interpretation. No spectra are fitted in this unit.

[MMB III, sections 2–3](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2011.19383.x)
reports channel spacing and the tabulated extrema across epochs.
[Official native MMB field contract](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/ReadMe/VIII/96?format=html&tex=true)
defines literal velocity fields and coordinates. All current source limits and
unknowns remain distinct from a measured physical star classification.
