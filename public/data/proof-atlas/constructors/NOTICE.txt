library-manifest.js, goedel-sample-manifest.js and recursion-examples-manifest.js.

AI-source environments retain their recorded compiled dependency revisions.

research/proof-atlas/recursion_fixture_source.py. Its catalogue can be replayed
without the private projects. Fresh independent controls are in
research/proof-atlas/verify_constructor_controls.py.
